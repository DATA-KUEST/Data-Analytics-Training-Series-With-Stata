# Think about it: sample answers

1. **Do-files vs Command window.** A do-file is a saved, re-runnable record of your analysis. You (or anyone else) can reproduce your results exactly, fix a mistake by editing one line and rerunning, and share the work. Commands typed in the Command window disappear once you close Stata (apart from the History window).

2. **Foreign vs domestic mpg.** No. The data show an association, not causation. Foreign cars in this 1978 sample may simply be smaller and lighter, and weight strongly affects fuel economy. Comparing like with like (for example, cars of similar weight) is the next step, which later lessons on regression will cover.
